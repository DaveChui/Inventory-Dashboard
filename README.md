# 📊 RetailIQ: Inventory Optimization Dashboard

> **A data-driven solution that helped reduce retail losses from KES 1.5M to 40K in 3 months**
---

## 🎯 The Problem

Retail businesses lose millions annually through:
- **Overstocking**: Capital tied up in slow-moving inventory
- **Stockouts**: Lost sales due to empty shelves  
- **Theft & Losses**: Preventable shrinkage
- **Poor Forecasting**: Reactive instead of predictive ordering

**Real Impact**: At my previous role, procedural losses exceeded KES 1.5M monthly.

---  ---

## ✅ The Solution

RetailIQ provides:
- **Real-time inventory health monitoring** across 50+ SKUs
- **Predictive reorder alerts** based on demand patterns
- **Loss prevention insights** highlighting high-risk products
- **ROI calculator** showing potential savings from optimization
- **Category performance analysis** for strategic decision-making

---

## 💰 Business Impact

Using this system, I achieved:
- ✅ **96% reduction in procedural losses** (KES 1.5M → 40K)
- ✅ **36% improvement in stock efficiency** (4.2 weeks → 2.68 weeks cover)
- ✅ **Optimized 300+ SKUs** across multiple categories
- ✅ **Projected annual savings**: KES 2M+ for medium-sized retailers

---

## 🛠️ Technical Stack

- **Backend**: Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- **Visualization**: Plotly, Streamlit
- **Analysis**: Statistical modeling, demand forecasting
- **Deployment**: Streamlit Cloud


## 📊 Features

### 1. **KPI Dashboard**
- Total revenue tracking
- Loss quantification (theft, stockouts, damages)
- Real-time savings opportunities

### 2. **Inventory Alerts**
- 🔴 Critical: Immediate reorder required
- 🟡 Warning: Low stock levels
- 🟢 Healthy: Optimal inventory

### 3. **Loss Prevention**
- High-theft product identification
- Pattern analysis by category
- Actionable prevention strategies

### 4. **ROI Calculator**
- Interactive savings projections
- What-if scenario modeling
- Monthly/annual impact forecasting

## 📈 Sample Insights

The dashboard reveals:
- **Electronics** category has highest revenue but also highest loss rate
- **Weekends** show 30% higher sales - optimal for promotions
- **Food** items require tighter stock management due to shorter shelf life
- **Seasonal patterns** (Q4, July) enable proactive inventory planning
